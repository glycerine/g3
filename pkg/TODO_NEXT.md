# g3 performance handoff

Updated November 27, 2026. The latest release saves 3.11% of complete examples alternating-bit instructions and 1.98% on Hybrid by declining small private premise selections in large batches. The preceding split_queue_2_new change saved 34.7%. The preceding release reuses ground witnesses from relation facts. It saves another 6.3% of complete POPL18 instructions, 9.9% on nested Ticket and 15.9% on test_liveness2. The earlier uniform Boolean proof saved about 8% on Hybrid. The arena stays. Each solver owns its mutable state and runs on one goroutine. The goal is to match or beat Z3 on every known workload, including complete GoIvy verification and workloads with many independent solvers. The gaps below remain open.
